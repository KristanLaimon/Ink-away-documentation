---
title: "Document surfaces"
description: "The rendering dependency contract used by external viewers."
---

A surface adapts your viewer's presentation to Ink Away. Event-only consumers
need no surface. Rendering consumers implement [the required contract](/docs/surfaces/required)
and may add [optional methods](/docs/surfaces/optional) for navigation, viewport
changes, reading direction, and discontinuous coordinate mapping.

The required contract page includes a minimal affine surface example. The
optional-method reference covers asynchronous handoffs and stale callbacks.
